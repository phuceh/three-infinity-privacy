Echo Grid - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Echo Grid is a puzzle game developed by Three Infinity. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Collection

Echo Grid does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, and does not share any data with third parties.

Local Storage

Game progress (completed levels, scores, and custom levels) is saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device.

Third Parties

Echo Grid does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
