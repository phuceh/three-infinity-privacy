Memorize Mandarin HSK 1–3 - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Memorize Mandarin is a Chinese vocabulary app for HSK 1–3 learners, developed by Three Infinity. It uses mnemonic stories, illustrations and audio to help you learn words. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

Using the App Without an Account

You can use Memorize Mandarin without creating or signing in to an account. When you do, the app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

Optional Account Sign-In

If you already have a Memorize Mandarin account, you can choose to sign in within the app to access the content included with your membership. Signing in is entirely optional.

When you sign in, your email address and password are sent securely to our authentication provider (Supabase) to verify your identity. The app then checks whether your account has an active membership. We use your email address only for signing in and checking your membership. It is not used for marketing within the app and is not shared with third parties for advertising.

Your sign-in session is stored on your device so you stay signed in. You can sign out at any time from the app's Settings.

Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by using "Request account deletion" in the app's Settings, or by emailing us at the address below. We will process deletion requests promptly.

Local Storage

Your learning progress, including words marked as learned, saved words, and whether you have unlocked the full app, is saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device. You can clear your learned and saved words at any time from the app's Settings.

Audio and Content

All vocabulary, illustrations and pronunciation audio are included within the app. The app does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, or location.

In-App Purchases

Memorize Mandarin offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock all words. This purchase is processed entirely by the app store. We do not receive or store your payment details, and the store's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Third Parties

Memorize Mandarin does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Supabase is used solely to provide optional account sign-in and membership checks.

Children's Privacy

Memorize Mandarin does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Use of the app without an account involves no personal data.
