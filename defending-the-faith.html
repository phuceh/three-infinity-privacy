Defending The Faith - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Defending The Faith is a Christian apologetics app developed by Three Infinity. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Collection

Defending The Faith does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account and does not share any data with third parties.

Local Storage

Your reading progress, favourites, personal notes, and settings (such as font size and theme) are saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share or export it.

Sharing and Exporting

If you choose to share a question, answer, note, or your progress, the app uses your device's built-in share feature. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you select where to share it.

In-App Purchases

Some versions of Defending The Faith (for example, on the Apple App Store) offer an optional one-time purchase to unlock all questions. These purchases are processed entirely by the app store. We do not receive or store your payment details, and the store's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Third Parties

Defending The Faith does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
