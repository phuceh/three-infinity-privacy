Word Tails - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Word Tails is a word puzzle game developed by Three Infinity. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data We Collect

Word Tails does not ask for or collect any personal information such as your name, email address or location. The app does not require an account.

Local Storage

Game progress (completed levels, stars, hints and settings) is saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Advertising

The free version of Word Tails shows ads provided through AppLovin MAX, which may show ads from AppLovin and its advertising partners. To show and measure ads, these providers may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and general information about how ads are viewed and tapped.

  • On iOS, the advertising identifier is only used for personalised ads if you allow tracking when the app asks. If you choose not to allow tracking, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.
  • Purchasing Word Tails Plus removes ads.

You can read how AppLovin handles this information at applovin.com/privacy.

In-App Purchases

Word Tails offers an optional one-time purchase, Word Tails Plus, which removes ads and unlocks unlimited custom games. Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details, and Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase. Your purchase is confirmed on your device with the App Store and can be restored at any time from the app's Settings.

Children

Word Tails is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads shown in the app are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Support

Need help with Word Tails, have a question about a purchase, or found a problem? Get in touch and we'll be happy to help.

  • Restoring Plus: open Settings (the cog on the home screen) and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Resetting progress: open Settings and tap Reset Progress.
